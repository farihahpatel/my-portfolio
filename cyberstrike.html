Welcome To CyberStrike

Cyberstrike is the prominent annual, intercollegiate tech festival organized by the Dot Com Club (DCC).

This features tech competitions, e-waste drives and gaming competitions such as FIFA, Valorant and BGMI.

DCC is a student collective from the Bsc. I.T and BVoc. Software Development departments of Jai Hind College, Mumbai.
